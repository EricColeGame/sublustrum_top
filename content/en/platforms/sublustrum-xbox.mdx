## Why Sublustrum Stayed PC-Only

The reasons are less dramatic than conspiracy theories suggest. During the era when Sublustrum launched, small studios typically shipped on a single platform, and the adventure genre's audience lived overwhelmingly on PC. Mouse-driven interfaces, inventory puzzles, and text-heavy storytelling translated awkwardly to gamepads at the time, and porting costs were hard to justify for a niche title with a modest marketing budget.

Publishing economics mattered too. A console release meant platform certification, physical manufacturing, regional ratings, and retail negotiations — overhead that a small team could rarely absorb. Many comparable adventure games of that period followed the same path: PC release, limited retail distribution, and no console follow-up.
