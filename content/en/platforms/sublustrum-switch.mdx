## What Is Sublustrum, and Why Does Anyone Care?

Sublustrum is a first-person point-and-click adventure game developed by the Russian studio Mandragora and originally released for Windows PC in the late 2000s. Players guide a lone protagonist through a dreamlike, half-deserted town, solving environmental puzzles while piecing together a fractured story that revolves around a lighthouse, a submarine, and a mounting sense of unease.

It is short, strange, and atmospheric. Players often compare its mood to Myst and its tension to Scratches — slow-burn dread rather than jump scares. That combination is exactly why people keep searching for it on modern hardware.
